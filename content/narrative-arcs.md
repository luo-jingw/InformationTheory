# Narrative arcs

Each episode begins with a concrete unresolved problem. Predictions receive explicit time; experiments identify their assumptions; the proof answers the opening question. Shared musical timbres support continuity, while each episode owns its notes, harmonic direction, transitions and resolutions.

| Episode | Problem and visible action | Payoff and musical direction |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Guess a weighted secret using yes/no questions; compare two question trees. | Frequent outcomes earn short paths; expected questions motivate entropy. A searching phrase contracts as the tree improves. |
| E02 | Compress the same stream with the wrong probability model; compare actual bit bills. | KL is the exact expected excess. Two initially competing phrases resolve only when the models match. |
| E03 | Try to recover a blurred secret by sharpening the observed data. | Processing alone cannot add information; side information changes the hypothesis. A degraded motif cannot recover missing notes without a new voice. |
| E04 | Put 100 messages into 1000 drawers; bet on any collision versus your card's ambiguity. | 99.40% somewhere versus 9.43% for one message. A question, held prediction, split reveal and local interruption lead into two separate proofs. |

| E06 | Remove spaces from a stream of codewords and attempt to decode it. | A prefix tree supplies boundaries without commas. Interrupted phrases become unambiguous as tree leaves appear. |
| E07 | Build a short average code by repeatedly merging the two rarest messages. | The exchange and induction arguments explain the greedy move. Paired musical fragments merge into larger phrases. |
